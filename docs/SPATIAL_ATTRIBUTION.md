# Spatial action attribution
Experimental association between a visually evidenced actor head (normalized image coordinates at release/action), independently reviewed local tracks, and separately timed recipient head at controlled reception. Uses all candidate tracks to detect local competition, rejects conflicting team/number, overlapping same-identity tracks, source revision mismatch and gaps; no automatic confirmation. Revision is a local file-stat token, not a content hash. Only uncropped original-time upload segments qualify. Requires fresh analysis, calibration and track reviews; older reports have no compatible coordinates or provenance. Pixel distance gates assume 16:9 and are provisional. Tracking still uses limited local windows; no whole-match identification claim. Report exports preserve original automatic event statistics and a separate candidate reattribution ledger. No model training or real-match validation in this change.
