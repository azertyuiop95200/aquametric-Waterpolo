# Metric mobility and dynamic calibration
## Integration
The legacy reviewed-track map no longer silently stops at 100 distinct frames or truncates the supplied tracks/points. It still receives the upstream local tracking sample, not the full ledger. Keep that distinction in the report. The reviewed-identity reducer now uses a sweep over interval boundaries, rejects incompatible overlaps at both endpoints, and measures distance, time-weighted speed median/P90 and time in each 2 m grid cell. Team heatmaps sum measured player-seconds; unknown identities are excluded. Both goals and the measurement coordinate frame are displayed. These are video-time observations, not regulation playing time. The speed/gap/conflict thresholds are engineering filters, not validated physiology.

## New multi-reference experimental module
Provide each reference with a manually reviewed image-to-water-plane anchor set expressed in one common coordinate frame, a static decor mask and field dimensions. SIFT matching and held-out pixel residual checks screen camera transforms; projections from different references must agree within the provisional 0.75 m tolerance. No automatic anchor discovery, interpolation of missing positions, identification, or validation of a water-contact position. A background-plane homography can be biased by parallax; metric ground truth remains necessary.

## Real Q1 audit
Existing private video and frame ledger, 25 fps, 19,793 frames. Five previously reviewed reference instants, 25 m length and assumed 20 m width. Uniform 10 s audit (80 requested and decoded frames), 76 with at least one accepted decor alignment, 75 with multiple references. 709 head-track candidate positions, 234 accepted candidate projections, 53 sample frames with at least one projection. Not an accuracy score or a continuous metric scan; neither candidates nor distances promoted to verified player statistics. Local tests: 6 calibration and 9 mobility tests passed on synthetic fixtures. For next steps: independently annotate water-plane landmarks across both halves, improve the head detector, validate reprojection error in metres, then run continuous calibrated tracking on a full match. Do not remove the remaining unmeasured status on this evidence.
