# Lineup exposure and numerical states
References: World Aquatics Competition Regulations, 18 February 2026, Part Six articles 4.1–4.2 (actual play and clock stoppages), 10.5.2 (ordinary exclusion re-entry at earliest applicable condition), https://www.worldaquatics.com/rules/competition-regulations . National competition profiles may differ; FFN https://www.ffnatation.fr/reglements-du-water-polo .
The default 480 s is a maximum validation bound, not a declaration of this match's period length. The reducer supports a configurable lower bound and at most four periods. The extraction schema requests independently evidenced complete, continuously observed lineups with actual-play clock endpoints. Clock intervals are unioned per team and period; disagreements remove the affected clock span. Exclusions require explicit evidence and are not assumed to last 18 s. Numerical states count all players including goalkeepers; no attacking team is inferred. No extrapolation across gaps or bench-time inference. Values are candidate estimates, not verified match statistics. Integrates into the existing video provider/runner/report and UI. Legacy runs require reanalysis for new observation fields. Synthetic fixtures test four periods, duplicates, contradictions, stoppages, early return, team separation and numerical intersections.
