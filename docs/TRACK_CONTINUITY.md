# Candidate continuity, not athlete identity
Short-gap association inspired by multi-object tracking literature: ByteTrack, ECCV 2022 (https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-20047-2_1). The implementation is not ByteTrack; it uses bidirectional endpoint motion, cap colour, mutual unambiguous association, and cut boundaries. Default thresholds are provisional engineering values. No missing sample is synthesized and no roster identity is assigned.
Water-polo-specific head detection and calibration dataset: https://pannaf.github.io/posts/sports-forecasting/water-polo-data ; inspect licensing and domain shift before use. Aquatic tracking background: https://www.wseas.com/journals/articles.php?id=4833 .
Real Q1 audit (private video not committed): 19,793 journal frames; 7,117 fragments; 408 proposed links; 6,709 candidate chains; 173,039 observations unchanged; 1,766 missing per-track samples not filled. A qualitative 16-pair review showed false positives on arms, bodies and water. No links have been promoted to verified identities; do not use this output to calculate physical or sporting statistics. Next: independent full-head annotations and domain-specific detection benchmark.
Exploratory Q1 head-point benchmark: 3 manually annotated frames (one partial ROI), 30 reference points, 29 predicted points, tolerance 20 px on 1280×720. One-to-one assignment: TP 17, FP 12, FN 13, precision 58.62%, recall 56.67%, F1 57.63%. Small, non-adjudicated sample; not an estimate of whole-match accuracy or a held-out model-training benchmark. The private images and annotations are not committed. Next, build independently reviewed stratified head/ball/cap annotations across full matches and evaluate a specialized detector. Use separate training/validation/test matches.
