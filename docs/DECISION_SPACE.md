# Spatial decision support
Uses simultaneous reviewed head-plane positions and candidate event attribution. Both goals are evaluated because actual attack direction is not established by existing global coordinates. Options show endpoint separation, segment distance to observed opponents, distance/angle gain, longitudinal gain, receiver proximity and fraction of 15 sampled straight horizontal rays outside assumed 0.6 m defender disks. Radius and discretization are engineering scenario parameters, not measured reach or scientific thresholds. Pareto status compares geometry without weighting or probability; no causal claim, no xG, no inferred failed-pass recipient, no legality/feasibility decision. Missing players, arm/ball height, goalie posture and movement remain unknown. The interactive view is a rotatable schematic perspective of measured planar positions with nominal goal frame, not 3D reconstruction of body/ball. 
Research basis: https://doi.org/10.3390/app12031298 (male elite goal-shot context, angles, passing and goalkeeper positioning); https://doi.org/10.1080/24748668.2017.1339258 (women's World Championship 2378 shots); https://doi.org/10.2478/hukin-2019-0107 (shot outcomes and defensive context); https://doi.org/10.3390/app12157856 (female throwing biomechanics). None validates the static disk model. Rules/profile dimensions must be verified for the relevant competition. Eight synthetic tests; independent real-match validation pending.
