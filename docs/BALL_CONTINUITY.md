# Ball temporal association
Experimental motion-gated, mutual unambiguous association of image candidates across the full frame ledger. No interpolation, camera compensation, ball verification, player control or pass inference. Ambiguity and cuts terminate tracks. Colour is weak evidence, not identity. Persistent means >=4 observations spanning >=0.12 s; this is an engineering filter, not a sporting fact.
Local replay of existing private Q1 ledger: 19,793 frames; 39,040 candidate observations retained; 32,989 proposed associations; 6,051 fragments; 2,154 persistent fragments; 15,888 frames containing persistent candidates, 6,106 with exactly one persistent candidate. 2,157 frames with competing candidate edges. Eight synthetic tests pass locally. These are diagnostic counts, not accuracy or football/water-polo event measurements. The original detector's false positives remain; an independently annotated ball dataset is required before promotion.
